What does this code do?

You'll work through pieces of the WebGL cube program, one at a time, with the comments removed. Each card has four short steps:

  1. Summarize what the code accomplishes and why it's needed.
  2. Check yourself: compare your summary with an example and tick off the key ideas you covered.
  3. Match comments to the lines they describe.
  4. Answer a few quick questions about the code.

There's no score. You decide what you got. Anything you missed goes on a review list at the end, so you know what to study.
